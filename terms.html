Terms of Use

Covers Kirtan, Dua and Amen. Last updated: 26 August 2026.

What the apps are

These are companions for personal devotional practice: a counter, a daily verse, a record of the days you kept. They are not a religious authority, and nothing in them is a ruling, a fatwa, a teaching, or spiritual direction. Where a question of practice matters to you, ask a teacher in your own tradition.

Scripture and translation

Scripture is reproduced from public-domain sources and public scripture APIs. Translations and transliterations are provided as an aid to recitation and may differ from the edition your tradition uses. Where the app shows a translation, the original is shown alongside it wherever the source provides one.

Subscriptions

  • Subscriptions are billed through your Google Play account and renew automatically until cancelled.
  • Cancel at any time in Google Play → Subscriptions. Cancelling stops the next renewal; the current period runs to its end.
  • Refunds are handled by Google Play under its own policy.
  • Everything central to practice — counting, streaks, the daily verse, reminders, all languages and themes — remains free. A subscription removes advertising and opens the additional tracks, longer counts, the pronunciation coach and unlimited saved verses.

Your content

Prayer tracks you record and reflections you write remain yours. They are stored on your device, and in our database only if you sign in and enable backup. We do not use them for anything else.

Acceptable use

Do not use the apps to harass others or to misrepresent the app as an official product of any religious institution. The circles feature, where enabled, is for encouragement between people who know each other.

No warranty

The apps are provided as they are. Reminders depend on your device's alarm and battery settings, which the app cannot override; voice counting depends on your device's speech recognition. Neither is guaranteed to work on every handset.
